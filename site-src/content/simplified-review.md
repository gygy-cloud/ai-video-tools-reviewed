## What Is Simplified?

Simplified is a single workspace that bundles four tools most creators pay for separately: a graphic design canvas, a video editor, an AI writer, and a social media scheduler. Its own market positioning is blunt about the ambition — it describes itself as an affordable alternative to using Canva and Buffer side by side.

The pitch matters because the fragmented-tool problem is real. A typical small team pays for a design tool, a scheduler, and an AI writing assistant, then loses hours a week moving assets between them. Simplified collapses that into one credit pool and one file library.

Two things are worth knowing up front. First, it's broad rather than deep — you are not getting a Resolve-grade video editor, you're getting a fast template-driven one. Second, the platform advertises a free entry point and a library of free AI tools, so you can evaluate the workflow before committing to a paid plan.

On scale, Simplified's own site claims **15 million+ active users**, **12+ native publishing integrations**, and a **G2 rating of 4.6 / 5 across 5,008+ reviews**. Those are the company's published figures — we have not independently audited them, but they are consistent across its pricing and affiliate pages.

## What the Affiliate Terms Actually Say

This is the part most Simplified reviews get wrong, including the version of this article we originally published. Simplified's affiliate program is genuinely attractive on paper — but its own pages do not agree on what the terms are.

Here is what we found by reading both official pages on **October 20, 2026**:

| Term | simplified.com/affiliate-program | affiliate.simplified.com (signup portal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission** | 40% recurring | 25% recurring |
| **Cookie window** | 30 days (main copy) — but **45 days** in the same page's FAQ | Not stated |
| **Commission duration** | Not stated anywhere | Not stated anywhere |
| **Minimum payout** | $50 **plus at least 2 referrals** | Not stated |
| **Payment methods** | PayPal and Wise | Not stated |

Two things stand out.

**First, the commission rate differs by 15 percentage points between the marketing page and the page you actually sign up on.** The marketing page leads with "Earn 40% recurring commission on every referral" and illustrates it with "if they spend $100, you earn $40." The FirstPromoter signup portal says 25%. One of these is wrong, or they apply to different plans, or the rate changed and one page wasn't updated. The program does not say which.

**Second, the cookie window is stated twice on the same page and disagrees with itself.** The main copy says a signup "within 30 days" earns commission; the FAQ says the window "is 45 days." Both are on `simplified.com/affiliate-program`.

**The duration is the bigger gap.** Neither official page says the commission lasts for the customer's lifetime. Simplified is frequently listed alongside lifetime-commission programs — but the word "lifetime" does not appear in its affiliate terms. If you are choosing a program because you were told it pays forever, confirm that in your affiliate dashboard before you build content around it.

**On verification status**: OpenAffiliate.dev lists Simplified as **community-submitted and not verified** (`verified: false`, `source: community`), with an explicit note that the program may run on FirstPromoter without a public terms page. That is not a knock on Simplified — it means nobody has confirmed the terms at source, which is exactly why the contradictions above exist.

## Key Features

### Design Canvas With Brand Kits

The design editor is the strongest part of the suite. Brand kits let you lock fonts, colors, and logos once, then apply them across every asset — which is the difference between "our social posts look consistent" and "our social posts look like five different companies made them."

One-click resizing takes a single design and reformats it for every platform aspect ratio. It's the feature that quietly saves the most time.

### AI Writing and Image Generation

Simplified's AI writer handles the usual long-form and ad-copy workflows, and its image generator produces usable stock-replacement visuals. Neither matches a dedicated best-in-class tool, but having them one tab away from your design canvas is the point.

### Video Editor With Templates

The video editor is template-first: pick a layout, drop in media, add captions and music. For talking-head clips, product promos, and social cutdowns, that's enough.

**Honest limitation**: it is not a timeline editor for narrative work. If you're cutting a 10-minute YouTube documentary with multi-track audio, you will outgrow it in a week.

### Social Media Scheduling

Scheduling is built in, and this is where the bundle earns its keep. Design → write the caption → schedule to every connected account, without exporting a PNG and uploading it somewhere else. Simplified advertises 12+ native publishing integrations.

## A Two-Week Trial, Step by Step

What follows is the realistic shape of a two-week evaluation, based on the documented workflow and published feature set — not a claim of a controlled benchmark.

**Days 1–3 — Setup.** Brand kit configuration is the first real task, and it takes roughly 20–30 minutes. Doing it properly pays off immediately: every new design starts on-brand by default, which is a genuine quality-of-life difference over starting from a blank canvas every time.

**Days 4–7 — Production.** Resizing carries most of the workload. One 1080×1080 design becomes six platform variants in a few minutes. A common surprise here is that the AI writer is stronger at short-form ad copy than at long-form drafts — the opposite of what most people expect from an "AI writer."

**Days 8–14 — Where it breaks.** The video editor is the ceiling. A product promo cuts together fine. Anything involving layered audio timing hits friction fast, and most evaluators finish that edit in a dedicated editor.

**The real takeaway** — scheduling inside the same tool as design is not a small convenience. Removing one export-upload-reformat cycle per post is the kind of saving that compounds across a week of publishing.

## FAQ

### Is Simplified worth it in 2026?

For solopreneurs and small teams currently paying for separate design and scheduling tools, yes — consolidation usually saves money and removes friction. At $79/mo the maths only works if it replaces at least two subscriptions. If you already own a professional design suite and a video editor you're happy with, you'll likely find it redundant.

### Does Simplified have a free plan?

Simplified advertises a free entry point and a library of free AI tools, but its pricing page does not publish a free plan tier alongside the paid ones. Treat "free" as a limited evaluation path and confirm what it includes before you plan around it.

### What is the best alternative to Simplified?

It depends on your bottleneck. **Canva** if design quality and ecosystem matter most, **Pictory AI** if your main job is turning long-form content into short video, and **Zebracat** if you're producing text-to-video ads at volume.

### Is Simplified good for video editing?

For short-form, template-driven content — social clips, product promos, captioned cutdowns — yes. For narrative editing with layered audio and precise timing, no. Budget for a dedicated editor alongside it.

### Can you make money promoting Simplified?

Yes, but verify the terms before you invest in content. Simplified's marketing page advertises **40% recurring**, while its signup portal says **25%**. The cookie window is stated as **30 days** in the main copy and **45 days** in the FAQ on the same page. No official page states a commission duration. Payout requires **$50 and at least 2 referrals**, via PayPal or Wise. Check all of these in your affiliate dashboard before publishing.

### Why does this review say the terms contradict themselves?

Because they do, on Simplified's own domain. `simplified.com/affiliate-program` states 40% and mentions both 30 and 45 days for the cookie; `affiliate.simplified.com` states 25%. We are reporting the conflict rather than picking the more flattering number.

## Final Verdict

Simplified's value proposition is consolidation, and in that lane it's genuinely strong. The design canvas, AI writer, and built-in scheduler form a coherent workflow, and $79/mo is easy to justify if it replaces two or three existing subscriptions.

It is not the best tool at any single job. If you want the sharpest design editor, buy one. If you want the deepest video editor, buy one. Simplified's case is that most creators don't need the sharpest or the deepest — they need one place where a post gets designed, written, scheduled, and shipped without three export steps in between.
