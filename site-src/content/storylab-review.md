## What StoryLab.ai Actually Is

Open the site and the navigation tells you the truth before the marketing copy does: the tools are named Video Idea, Video Script, Video Hook, Video Brief, YouTube Title, YouTube Description, YouTube Tags, Video SEO, Video Promotion, YouTube Thumbnail Maker. There is no render button anywhere in that list.

StoryLab writes the **words and metadata** a video needs — the hook that decides whether someone keeps watching, the script structure, the title that decides whether it gets found — and stops there. If you expect an MP4, you will be disappointed within ten minutes.

The rest is broad for the price: blog outlines, email subject lines, ad copy, eBooks, a campaign builder, a text-to-image generator, a content evaluator. Around thirty generators in one login. The homepage claims "Trusted by 100,000+ marketers" and "over 1 million content pieces created" — both self-reported, neither verifiable against an independent platform.

One practical note: the marketing site's `/pricing/` path returns a 404. Real pricing lives at `app.storylab.ai/pricing` and renders client-side, which is why secondhand write-ups quote prices that are years stale.
